# R-01 Outreach Execution Kit — Multi-Agent Squad

> **Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Status:** READY FOR OWNER EXECUTION · **Companions:** `participant-source-map-2026-08-11.md` (channels), `recruitment-outreach-template.md` (base email), `participant-screener.md` (quota/disqualification)
> **Rule:** The owner/team executes outreach and returns only anonymized outcomes. This kit removes every remaining blocker except the actual outreach.

## A. Per-channel message variants (use with the base template)

### A1. Conference / summit contact (CFO, Controller, FP&A cohorts)
**Subject:** Research conversation: finance close, planning, and board reporting workflow

> Hello [Name],
> We saw you [speaking at / attending] [Conference] and are researching how finance teams actually run close, forecasting, reconciliations, and board reporting today — we are not selling a product. We are building a public evidence base of real workflows and would value a 60-minute anonymized session with a [CFO / controller / FP&A leader] at a multi-entity organization.
> Would you be open to that, or to pointing us to a colleague who fits? We will not request confidential financial values or materials.

### A2. Professional community post (r/FPandA + Discord/Slack, FP&A cohort)
**Subject (r/FPandA):** Research participants wanted: FP&A workflow study (anonymized, no sales)

> We are researchers building a public evidence base on how FP&A teams handle forecasting, variance, and reporting — not selling anything. We're recruiting working FP&A leads/analysts at multi-entity companies for one anonymized 60-minute session about your last real forecast/close cycle.
> If interested, reply or DM; we'll screen for fit and capture consent before anything else. Happy to share the anonymized aggregate findings back with the community afterward.
> [Mod-approved; no compensation beyond transparency; no product pitch.]

### A3. Association / council ask (Controllers Council, AFP, ISACA)
**Subject:** Member research study: close, consolidation, and finance-systems workflow

> Hello [Chapter/Council contact],
> We are conducting an anonymized practitioner study on close/consolidation and finance-systems deployment (controllers, FP&A, IT/security). Would [Council/Chapter] be open to us posting a recruitment notice to members, or suggesting 2–3 member contacts who fit?
> Deliverable: anonymized aggregate findings shared back with the community.

### A4. Implementation partner ask (EPM consultancies, Big-4 alumni)
**Subject:** Practitioner research: EPM implementation and close transformation

> Hello [Name],
> We are researching implementation burden and close/consolidation transformation across EPM platforms (OneStream, Anaplan, Pigment, Hyperion, etc.). Your public practice materials show deep delivery experience. Would you (or a delivery lead) be open to a 60-minute anonymized session on a recent implementation — timeline, cost drivers, admin burden, and what customers struggled with most?
> We will not solicit business; findings are anonymized and shared back.

## B. Scheduling playbook

1. On reply, send a 1-line fit check (role + org shape + recent workflow) — no screener dump.
2. Offer 3 concrete 60–90-min slots; state the session is recorded only with consent, otherwise notes-only.
3. Send calendar invite + a "no preparation needed" note (we want the real workflow, not a polished story).
4. 24h before: send the `research-session-notes-template.md` briefing line: "We'll start with your most recent [close/forecast/board cycle]."
5. After: same-day evidence-log entry (E-###), anonymized; send thank-you + aggregate-findings promise.

## C. Screener quick-reference (full: participant-screener.md)

| Check | Pass |
|---|---|
| Role fits cohort + decision/hands-on authority | yes |
| Multi-entity, Excel-heavy, incumbent EPM, or regulated context (≥1 of 4) | yes |
| Can describe a recent real workflow (last 90 days) | yes |
| No conflict: not paid to endorse a competitor/solution | yes |
| Consent for anonymized capture | yes (outside repo) |
| Vertical cap ≤40% of sample | yes |
