# Primary Research Participant Screener — FinPlan Pro

> **Purpose:** Recruit evidence-bearing participants for the controlled close-to-decision-to-board-pack hypothesis. Do not recruit only friendly users or people who already like the proposed solution.

## Required sample

| Cohort | Target interviews | Must have done recently | Exclude if |
|---|---:|---|---|
| CFO / VP Finance | 4–5 | approved budget, reforecast, board review, or finance-system spend | no influence on process/tooling decision |
| Controller / consolidation lead | 4–5 | month-end close, reconciliation, certification, audit support | only theoretical/consulting knowledge |
| FP&A lead / analyst | 4–5 | forecast/model update and variance narrative | no hands-on planning workflow |
| IT / security / enterprise architect | 3–4 | reviewed finance-data SaaS or endpoint/deployment architecture | cannot discuss policy at a useful level |
| Implementation partner / finance transformation lead | 2–3 | deployed or replaced a planning/close platform | vendor-only sales role without delivery experience |

## Segment quotas

- At least 6 multi-entity organizations.
- At least 4 organizations using Excel as a material close/planning control surface.
- At least 4 with an incumbent EPM/FP&A platform or active replacement evaluation.
- At least 3 regulated/data-residency-sensitive organizations.
- No more than 40% from one vertical before the initial ICP decision.

## Screening questions

1. What are your role, decision authority, and hands-on responsibilities for close, planning, reporting, or finance systems?
2. How many legal entities, currencies, finance users, and major source systems are involved?
3. Which tools are used for ERP, planning, consolidation, reporting, and workflow today?
4. Describe the last close/reforecast/board cycle. Where did manual reconciliation, export, approval chase, or data-quality rework occur?
5. What business consequence results from a late, incorrect, or unexplainable number?
6. What security/deployment constraints apply to financial data and endpoint/local storage?
7. Are you evaluating/using a platform such as Anaplan, OneStream, Pigment, Adaptive, Planful, Vena, or an Excel-centric alternative? Why?
8. Would you permit a 60–90 minute workflow observation or sanitized artifact walkthrough?

## Disqualification rules

- Cannot describe a recent real workflow.
- No meaningful financial data/process responsibility.
- Cannot consent to non-sensitive observation or discussion.
- Vendor representative whose compensation depends on a particular product outcome, unless explicitly classified as implementation-partner evidence.

## Bias controls

- Ask for last actual event before showing any FinPlan concept.
- Recruit incumbent users and non-users equally.
- Include at least two participants who rejected or replaced a planning/close platform.
- Record negative evidence verbatim; do not recode objections as feature requests.
- Never claim a participant quote represents the market without sample context.
