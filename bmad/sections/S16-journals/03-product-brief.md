# S16 — Product Brief

**Date:** 2026-07-25

## 1. Vision
Browse and audit every journal entry.

## 2. Target Users
- Auditors, accountants.

## 3. Problem & Value
- Problem: no filtered view of postings.
- Value: auditability.

## 4. Success
- Filter by account/date; paginated.

## 5. Scope
- In: journals list. Out: edit postings (immutable by design).
