# Research Recruitment Outreach Template — FinPlan Pro

> **Purpose:** Recruit independent evidence, not pitch or sell a pre-decided product.

## Email / message template

**Subject:** Research conversation: finance close, planning, and reporting workflow

Hello [Name],

We are researching how finance teams currently handle close, forecasting, reconciliations, and board reporting. We are not asking you to buy or endorse a product. We want to understand the real workflow: what systems are used, where effort or risk accumulates, and what would make a change worthwhile.

Would you be open to a 60-minute research session? We are especially interested in a recent real close, reforecast, or board-pack process. We will not request credentials, confidential financial values, or materials you cannot share. Findings will be anonymized.

We are seeking perspectives from [CFO / Controller / FP&A / IT-security] leaders at multi-entity organizations. If you are a fit, please reply with:

1. Your role and involvement in the workflow;
2. The systems used today;
3. Whether you can discuss a recent process at a non-sensitive level;
4. Any deployment/security requirements relevant to finance systems.

Thank you,
[Researcher]

## Recruitment integrity rules

- Do not promise future features, price, or roadmap dates.
- Do not recruit only supporters of local-first, AI, or a specific competitor position.
- Do not offer leading descriptions of the proposed solution before current workflow observation.
- Disclose any commercial relationship with participant/employer.
- Obtain consent before recording; keep notes anonymized.
- Add every completed session—including negative outcomes—to the evidence log.
