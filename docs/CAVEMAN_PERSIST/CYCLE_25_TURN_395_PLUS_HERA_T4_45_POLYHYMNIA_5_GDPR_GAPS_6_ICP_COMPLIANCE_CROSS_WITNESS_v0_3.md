### 2.2 Vera 7.0/10 P0A-09 Gap Root Cause Analysis (3 root causes)

**ROOT CAUSE 1**: NO demonstrability test per Art. 7(1) GDPR
- Art. 7(1) requires: "the controller shall be able to demonstrate that the data subject has consented"
- Polyhymnia 03-CONSENT-CAPTURE.md §4.1 specifies `consentRegistry.capture()` BUT no test asserting that captured consent is **retrievable + verifiable** by DSAR or audit
- Vera 7.0/10 because: legal demonstrability is THE P0A-09 control, missing test = control is unverified

**ROOT CAUSE 2**: `uiElementId` not REQUIRED in `consentRegistry.capture()` payload
- Current spec (Polyhymnia v0.1): `uiElementId: string` (optional)
- Justitia requirement: `consentUIElementId: string` **REQUIRED** (non-optional) for Art. 7(1) demonstrability
- UI element ID = DOM element clicked = PRIMARY evidence of consent action per EDPB Guidelines §63

**ROOT CAUSE 3**: NO EDPB Guidelines §60-§82 reference
- EDPB Guidelines 05/2020 on consent under Regulation 2016/679 §60-§82 are **the authoritative interpretation** of Art. 6(1)(a) + Art. 7
- §63 explicit consent, §67 freely given, §72 specific, §82 withdrawal
- Polyhymnia doc cites Art. 6 + Art. 7 but NOT EDPB §60-§82 — Vera 7.0/10 because: regulatory mapping incomplete
