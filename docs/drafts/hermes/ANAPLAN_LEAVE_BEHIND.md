## The CFO's 30-second takeaway

> "Anaplan is the right tool for the Fortune 1000. **FinPlan Pro is the right tool for the 50-500 FTE band Anaplan's $400K bill excludes.** Year 1 we save $260K-$510K, year 2+ we save $50K-$200K, and our scenarios run in 30 seconds instead of 3 weeks. **The data lives on our laptop, not in Anaplan's cloud, and we own the file.** That's a 6-12 month implementation compressed into a 30-min install."
