# Workday (Multi-Suite Platform) Battlecard — Sales Playbook for ICP-1 (Carla, CFO Mid-Market, Enterprise-Grade)

> **Frame for the cycle:** Workday is the **enterprise HCM + Financials + Adaptive Planning + Extend multi-suite platform** that large mid-market to enterprise CFOs pick when they want to consolidate HR + Finance + Planning in one vendor. It's the **safe, big-budget, multi-suite pick for ICP-1 Carla** at the enterprise-scale $200K+ deals. This battlecard is the sheet the AE needs when the buyer says "we're standardizing on Workday across the company" or "our CHRO chose Workday, and we have to pick Workday for FP&A too." The 3 most damaging objections and 3 strongest counter-moves are at §6.

> **Differentiation from T-HER-014b (Adaptive):** The Adaptive card is for buyers considering **standalone Adaptive Planning** (the planning piece only). This Workday card is for buyers considering the **full Workday multi-suite platform** (HCM + Financials + Planning + Extend). The conversation is different: standalone Adaptive is a $50K-$500K FP&A decision; full Workday is a $500K-$5M enterprise platform decision that includes HR, payroll, financials, and planning.

## 1. Workday at a glance

Workday, Inc. is the **enterprise HCM + Financials + Adaptive Planning + Extend multi-suite cloud platform** founded in 2005 by Dave Duffield (co-founder of PeopleSoft) and Aneel Bhusri (current CEO) [1]. IPO 2012 on NASDAQ; FY2024 revenue **$7.26B** [2]. 10,000+ enterprise customers as of 2024 (including ~50% of Fortune 50) [3]. Workday positions itself as "the AI platform for HR + Finance" — the multi-suite consolidation story is the wedge. The platform includes Workday HCM, Workday Financials (GL/AP/AR), Workday Adaptive Planning (acquired 2018 for ~$1.55B), Workday Extend (low-code platform), Workday Student, and Workday Illuminate (AI). **Pricing is NOT publicly disclosed** — every quote requires a sales call. 10-K filings + analyst reports infer typical large-enterprise deals of **$500K-$5M/yr for the full multi-suite**; standalone HCM-only deals can be $200K-$1M/yr [4].

> **The one-line summary for the buyer:** "Workday is the right answer if you're a 500+ employee enterprise consolidating HR + Finance + Planning in one vendor, you have $500K+ to spend, and you have 6-12 months for a phased rollout. It's the wrong answer if you're pre-Series-B, you don't have Workday HCM, and you want a 30-min install."

## 2. The 5 weaknesses of Workday (with evidence)

### 2.1 Massive cost — $500K-$5M/yr for full multi-suite, plus 6-12 month rollout

- **What the buyer feels:** "Workday quoted us $2.4M/yr for HCM + Financials + Adaptive Planning. Plus the implementation partner was $1.8M. Plus we needed 4 FTE for 12 months to migrate. Total Year-1 cost was $6M+." Workday's pricing is **inferred from 10-K filings, analyst reports, and customer-disclosed numbers** [4]. Typical large-enterprise multi-suite deals are **$500K-$5M/yr license + $500K-$2M implementation partner fees** = $1M-$7M Year-1 total cost of ownership.
- **The number:** Workday's 10-K FY2024 reports $7.26B revenue, 10,000+ customers = **avg $725K/customer** [2]. HONEST LABELING: Workday does NOT publish per-product pricing; numbers inferred with **+/- 30% confidence** [4]. Large-enterprise multi-suite deals at the top end are $5M+/yr.
- **The FinPlan Pro counter:** FinPlan Pro Business tier at $499/user/mo for 100 users = **$598,800/yr**, plus optional $25K flat-rate implementation. **Year-1 savings: $400K-$6M+ vs typical Workday deal** [PRICING.md]. For Carla at the $50K-$100K/yr ICP-1 mid-market, Pro is in range; for the large-enterprise variant at $200K+, Business is the path. Workday is over-scoped for the mid-market Carla buyer.

### 2.2 6-12 month rollout — partner-required, multi-suite complexity

- **What the buyer feels:** "We signed Workday in Q1. HCM went live in Q4. Financials went live Q1 next year. Adaptive Planning went live Q3 next year. Total 18 months. The CFO quit. The new CFO wants to switch to Pigment + Rippling + FinPlan Pro." Workday's typical multi-suite rollout is **6-12 months HCM-only, 12-18 months full multi-suite** with a certified Workday partner [5]. Self-serve is not an option.
- **The number:** Workday's published implementation guide is 240+ pages across HCM/Financials/Adaptive [5]. G2 reviews consistently cite 6-18 month implementations with $500K-$2M partner fees.
- **The FinPlan Pro counter:** Same as the Adaptive + Anaplan counter — FinPlan Pro ships **all 200+ engines, 17 sector presets, and the AI Copilot in the binary** — `ONBOARDING.md` (Mnemosyne P0, 30-min) covers 80% of the SaaS-CFO use case. No implementation line item. Optional paid implementation sprint at flat $25K is the only consultant-tier option.

### 2.3 Multi-suite lock-in — HCM + Financials + Planning all-in on one vendor

- **What the buyer feels:** "We're all-in on Workday HCM + Financials + Adaptive. The day we want to swap any one piece, we have to swap all three. The integration is the lock-in vector." Workday's strategy post-Adaptive acquisition is to **bundle HCM + Financials + Adaptive + Extend + Illuminate into a single platform** [6]. Customers report that swapping any one piece (e.g., Adaptive for Pigment) breaks integrations across all three.
- **The number:** Workday's 10-K FY2024 reports 10,000+ customers; the "full multi-suite" customer count is the strategic KPI [2]. Workday's earnings calls emphasize "consolidation" and "single source of truth" — the lock-in is by design.
- **The FinPlan Pro counter:** FinPlan Pro is **HR-system-agnostic + finance-system-agnostic + planning-agnostic** — works with Workday HCM, BambooHR, Rippling, Gusto, ADP, NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, or no other systems at all. Per `docs/drafts/architecture/decisions/ADR-007-masterStorage.md` (data-residency decisions). Vera/Carla can swap HR/Finance systems without re-implementing FinPlan Pro. **The FinPlan Pro philosophy: we win the FP&A seat, not the multi-suite consolidation.**

### 2.4 Workday Illuminate AI is HCM-focused, not FP&A-focused

- **What the buyer feels:** "Workday Illuminate AI is mostly HCM/HR-focused (recruiting, talent, learning). The FP&A-specific AI features are limited to Adaptive's 'narrative summaries' and 'anomaly flags' — not what we expected from a $2.4M/yr spend." Workday Illuminate's strategy is **HCM/HR-first AI**; the Adaptive-specific AI features are the same ones covered in T-HER-014b Adaptive [7].
- **The number:** Workday Illuminate's published FP&A-specific features (as of 2026-06-13) are: anomaly flagging + narrative summaries + forecast suggestions (delegated to Adaptive). No purpose-built FP&A Copilot, no audit-trail-on-AI.
- **The FinPlan Pro counter:** Same as the Adaptive counter — FinPlan Pro ships an **AI Copilot at the $0 OSS tier** with 100 queries/day, full audit trail, natural-language scenario modeling. The audit-trail-on-AI is the differentiator for ICP-1 Carla's SOX/SOC-2 conversations.

### 2.5 Slow feature velocity for the planning piece (Adaptive is Workday's second priority)

- **What the buyer feels:** "Workday ships HR features every quarter. Adaptive ships planning features every 6-9 months. The planning piece feels like a second-class citizen inside the multi-suite." Adaptive is **Workday's secondary product** vs HCM/Financials [8]. Workday's R&D investment is heavily weighted to HCM + Illuminate; Adaptive's roadmap is slower.
- **The number:** Workday's 10-K reports R&D spend of $2.6B in FY2024; the breakdown by product is not disclosed but HCM + Illuminate are the strategic priorities [2]. G2 reviews flag "slow feature updates" in Adaptive-specific reviews [8].
- **The FinPlan Pro counter:** FinPlan Pro ships **200+ engines, 17 sector presets, AI Copilot at $0 OSS, 7-year Object Lock** — every quarter we ship net-new functionality because FP&A is our only product. **The FinPlan Pro philosophy: focus is the feature.** Single-product vendors ship faster than multi-suite platforms on the focused product.

## 4. When Workday wins (be honest — 5 scenarios)

Workday is the right answer in **5 specific scenarios**:

1. **Buyer is a 500+ employee enterprise standardizing on Workday HCM + Financials + Adaptive** — Workday's bundle discount + SSO + native data sync + the multi-suite consolidation story is genuinely the right pick.
2. **Buyer's board mandates a "single vendor for HR + Finance + Planning"** — Workday's multi-suite story is real; FinPlan Pro's HR/Finance-agnostic design is a different value prop.
3. **Buyer has $500K-$5M budget and 6-12 month rollout tolerance** — Carla's $50K-$100K ICP-1 mid-market budget is below Workday's enterprise floor; for the large-enterprise $200K+ variant, Workday is the right tool.
4. **Buyer has complex multi-suite requirements: HCM + Payroll + Financials + Planning + Extend in one platform** — Workday's full-stack coverage is real; FinPlan Pro is FP&A-only by design.
5. **Buyer needs Workday-ILM-certified AI for talent + HR + planning** — Workday Illuminate is the gold standard for HCM/HR AI; Adaptive is the planning layer on top.

> **Honest framing for the AE:** "Workday is the right tool if you're 500+ employees consolidating HR + Finance + Planning in one vendor and you have $500K+ to spend. We're the right tool if you're pre-Series-B, you want a 30-min install, and the words 'open file format' + 'audit-trail-on-AI' are on your auditor's Q3 list. Or if you're a 100-person company with $80K-$150K FP&A budget who doesn't need the full Workday HCM stack."

## 6. Objection handling (the 3 most damaging)

### Objection 1: "Workday is the safe pick. Our board will approve it."

**The real objection:** Career risk for the buyer. "If I pick Workday and it fails, no one questions me. If I pick FinPlan Pro and it fails, I'm the one who picked the unknown vendor."

**The counter:**

- **The OSS reset:** "Workday is a $7B-revenue public company. We are OSS — the codebase is on GitHub, the .fpa file is open. If FinPlan Pro goes bankrupt, the OSS continues. That's the safest possible 'safe pick' — your data and your tooling survive us."
- **The reference-ability angle:** "If your board wants 'enterprise-grade', show them the OSS + audit-trail-on-AI + 7-year Object Lock. The same SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / GDPR DPA controls that audit Workday audit us. The 'safe' is in the controls, not the vendor's market cap."
- **The pilot reset:** "Pilot FinPlan Pro on one business unit for 30 days. Run Workday Adaptive in parallel on another. Compare audit trail, model flexibility, AI output, time-to-first-model. The pilot is the proof. No board will question a 30-day pilot."

### Objection 2: "Workday integrates with our HCM, payroll, financials, and planning out of the box."

**The real objection:** Integration cost. "If I pick something that doesn't integrate with our Workday stack, my team will spend 12 months on data sync."

**The counter:**

- **The HR/Finance/Planning-agnostic reset:** "We integrate with Workday HCM + Financials, BambooHR, Rippling, NetSuite, QuickBooks, Xero, and 12 other systems. The integration is the same: 1 CSV import or 1 REST API call. We don't have a Workday-bundled discount; we have a Workday-quality integration that costs the same as the BambooHR one."
- **The future-proofing reset:** "Workday's multi-suite integration is great today. It's also a 5-10 year lock-in to Workday's roadmap. If Workday deprecates an API, all multi-suite customers are stuck. Our HR/Finance/Planning-agnostic design means we follow the market, not a single vendor's roadmap."
- **The data-portability reset:** "Workday's integration is via Workday's data export. Your data is still in Workday's cloud. Our .fpa file is the data + the model + the audit trail in a single open binary. If you leave us, you take everything."

### Objection 3: "Workday's multi-suite consolidation is the right architecture for the long term."

**The real objection:** Architectural lock-in via integration density. "If I pick a single-vendor that doesn't consolidate HR + Finance + Planning, my team will spend years on integrations."

**The counter:**

- **The single-product focus reset:** "Workday's multi-suite consolidation is one architecture. The FinPlan Pro architecture is **best-of-breed FP&A + best-of-breed HR + best-of-breed finance + best-of-breed planning** connected by open APIs. You pick the best vendor for each layer. The integration cost is 1 CSV import per system, not a 5-year multi-suite consolidation roadmap."
- **The 80/20 reset:** "Workday's multi-suite covers 100% of HR + Finance + Planning. The 80% of the FP&A use case is covered by FinPlan Pro at $99/user/mo. The 20% long-tail (HCM + payroll + GL consolidation) is covered by Workday. The best-of-breed architecture costs 50-80% less than the multi-suite consolidation."
- **The OSS reset:** "Workday's multi-suite is closed-source. Our FP&A piece is OSS. You can audit the FP&A code, fork it, run a self-hosted version. The FP&A layer survives our bankruptcy. The HCM + Finance + Planning layers survive Workday's bankruptcy. The architecture is more resilient than the multi-suite consolidation."
