2. **Anaplan partnership disclosure (Q2):** If Baker Tilly has an Anaplan partnership, is it exclusive (revenue-share + co-marketing) or non-exclusive (referral)? If exclusive, the channel motion is conflicted by design — Baker Tilly Beth is structurally biased toward Anaplan, and we cannot rely on her as a neutral FinPlan channel partner. (Source: Baker Tilly public partner directory + Anaplan partner page; Hermes to verify by 2026-09-15.)
3. **FP&A-services-led vs audit-led (Q3):** Per `PHASE_1_GTM.md` v0.3.1 §8 Q7 selection criterion #2, partner's primary buyer-relationship motion must be FP&A transformation, not audit. 4. **Partner-PIC profile (Q4):** Is Beth the formal partner-PIC at Baker Tilly with a published bio, and does her practice area overlap with our Vera (ICP-2) Anaplan-replacement motion? If Beth is a junior associate or a partner in a non-FP&A practice area, the LOI does not anchor a real motion. (Source: Baker Tilly public partner directory + LinkedIn; Iris T-IR-014 to validate by 2026-06-30.)

**Verdict matrix (4 questions × 4 verdict options):** If ANY of Q1/Q2/Q3/Q4 returns EXCLUSIVE-ANAPLAN or NO-DATA, the Baker Tilly channel motion is structurally conflicted. **Fallback:** downgrade Beth Y2 base to $120K (Risk 10 fire-control) and re-cut to direct-only Y2 (Carla + Vera + Chris). **If all 4 return NEUTRAL or PREFERRED-ANAPLAN-with-disclosure**, proceed to §3 tier-2 partner selection.

If Risk 10 materializes (2 channel conflicts escalate to Founder by 2027-Q1), the strategic fallback is **downgrade Beth Y2 base to $120K floor** and re-cut to direct-only Y2 (Carla + Vera + Chris, no Beth). This is the cleanest break because:

1. **Y2 base drops to $3.6M** ($3.9M - $300K Beth = $3.6M, vs Y2 stretch $6.5M still achievable via Carla + Vera + Chris expansion)
2. **Y2 floor stays at $1.8M** ($2.4M - $120K Beth + $480K partial-Vera recovery = $1.8M, vs Y1-anchor floor $660K)
3. **Channel motion becomes Y3+** — defer Baker Tilly + 4 tier-1 partners to Y3 (2028) when direct-sales motion is mature enough to absorb channel-conflict risk
4. **Founder time recovers** — 4-8h/week founder channel time re-allocated to Vera ICP-2 founder-led demos (which have higher per-deal ARR: $80K Vera vs $60K Beth)
5. **Strategic narrative re-cuts** — Y3 board pack (Nov 2027) tells the story as "channel motion deferred to Y3 because direct-sales motion outpaced channel-program maturity"

**Trigger conditions (all 3 must hold):**

- (a) 2+ channel conflicts escalate to Founder by 2027-Q1

- (c) Baker Tilly Q1 2026 LOI conversion rate <15% by 2027-Q1 (vs 30-60% baseline per §3)
