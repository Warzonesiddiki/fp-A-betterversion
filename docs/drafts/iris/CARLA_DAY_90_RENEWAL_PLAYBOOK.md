## §1. The 3-Question Day-90 Check-in (CFO-flavored)

CSM + AE (joint call) opens Calendly, dials Carla, asks verbatim:

> **Q1 — "Walk me through the last 90 days. What are the 3 board-pack moments where our tool changed a decision the CEO made?"**
>
> - **PASS:** 3 specific moments named, with the decision-attribution. ("The Acme deal close-miss scenario, the Q3 hiring freeze what-if, the November board pack — T-INFERRED common pattern.")
> - **YELLOW:** 1-2 specific moments, but the decision-attribution is fuzzy. ("I think it influenced the Q3 close.")
> - **RED:** "I'm not sure" or "the tool has been useful but I can't point to a specific decision" (Stage 6 Habit not landed; save motion needed).
>
> **Q2 — "If you had to put a dollar value on the time your Controller saved in the last 90 days, what would it be? And the decisions you made faster?"**
>
> - **PASS:** Specific dollar amount + decision-attribution. ("$50K in Controller time saved; 2 weeks faster on the Q3 close.")
> - **YELLOW:** Vague dollar amount or no decision-attribution. ("It's saved us a lot of time.")
> - **RED:** "I don't know" or "I'd have to ask the Controller" (Carla is not yet anchored; this is the renewal risk).
>
> **Q3 — "You're up for renewal in 30 days. There are 3 paths: (a) renew at current seat count, (b) add Controller seats + scenario seats, (c) multi-year contract. Which one matches your CFO planning cycle? And separately — can I ask you to introduce us to 5 peer CFOs in your network this quarter, even if the intro is a 1-line email forward?"**
>
> - **PASS:** Specific path chosen (a/b/c) + says "yes, send me the 5-blurb" (5-warm-intro motion starts here; the 5-wait-lost motion starts at the same time).
> - **YELLOW:** Path chosen but 5-warm-intro is "let me think" (30-day re-prompt; do not let this slip into Q+1).
> - **RED:** Path is "we're evaluating" or "we're not sure we'll renew" (renewal is at risk; trigger save motion from §5).

**The 3 questions take 12-15 minutes.** The remaining 30-33 minutes are value-summary slide co-build (T-IR-018 template) + multi-year math walkthrough + 5-warm-intro plan. CSM T-IR-004 currently has no spec for this; **45 min is the new allocation** (30 was wrong; 60 is too long for a CFO). **Channel = Zoom, NOT Slack DM** (vs Chris T-IR-017 §6 which is also call-first, but Chris is async-friendly; Carla is call-only).

## §2. The 4 Renewal Trigger Gates (the "is the renewal on track?" detector)

The 4 gates CSM + AE verify in the customer's workspace **before hanging up**, with Carla (and optionally the Controller):

| #      | Gate                                                                                          | Day-90 target | Owner               | Below-target = RED                                                              |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **G1** | **5 saved board-pack templates** (re-runnable across quarters)                                | Day 80        | Carla               | <3 saved templates; "I rebuild the board pack each quarter"                     |
| **G2** | **CEO has seen ≥3 board packs** (audit-log: board pack events with CEO's email)               | Day 85        | Carla + CEO         | <2 board packs exported; CEO not in the share-list                              |
| **G3** | **3+ "I taught someone else" events** (Controller taught Controller; Carla taught VP Finance) | Day 88        | Carla OR Controller | <2 events; tool is still single-team-scope                                      |
| **G4** | **5-warm-intro commitment captured** (Carla agrees to forward blurb to 5 peers)               | Day 90        | Carla               | "I'm not sure I can commit to 5" (the 5-LOI motion is at risk for next quarter) |

**G1 + G2 is the gate** (not G3 like Chris). Per JOURNEY_MAP_CARLA.md Stage 7, the renewal is "earned" when the **CEO has seen 3 board packs AND Carla has 5 saved templates** — meaning the tool is institutional, not personal. If G1 is GREEN but G2 is RED, the tool is Carla's notepad, not the company's FP&A platform. Renewal is at risk.

**If G2 is RED:** The intervention is a 30-min joint Carla-CEO call within 14 days (NOT a Carla-only call). Per JOURNEY_MAP_CARLA.md Stage 6 GO WRONG, the CEO has not seen the tool because the Controller has built the export but the CEO has not been invited. CSM + AE opens the door; Carla walks the CEO through.

**If G4 is RED but G1-G3 GREEN:** The 5-warm-intro motion is blocked. CSM pivots to **industry-event warm intros** (same fallback as T-IR-021b §2 S5 RED) — r/FPandA, SaaStr CFO Summit, CFO Leadership Council. Offer Carla a **"CFO reference program"** slot (T-INFERRED: quarterly call with our founder + 2-3 other reference CFOs; needs Founder ratification per T-IR-021a §3 + §8).

## §3. Renewal-Intent Signals (Positive + Negative)

**Positive signals (renewal is on track):**

- ✅ Carla mentions the tool in a peer-CFO conversation unprompted (T-INFERRED: >3 mentions in 90 days = strong signal)
- ✅ Controller asks about advanced features (T-INFERRED: 2+ "how do I..." questions about scenario modeling, multi-entity consolidation)
- ✅ CEO asks for the tool to be rolled out to a sister-entity or business unit (seat-expansion signal)
- ✅ Carla asks about multi-year pricing BEFORE the renewal conversation (strongest signal)
- ✅ Carla proactively offers to be a reference customer (the 5-warm-intro motion is then trivial)

**Negative signals (renewal is at risk):**

- 🚨 "We're evaluating other tools" (immediate AE escalation; do NOT wait for the renewal call)
- 🚨 "The Controller has been doing this manually" (signal that the tool is not in daily use; reset to Day-7 motion)
- 🚨 "The CEO has not seen the board pack" (G2 RED; trigger CEO-invite intervention)
- 🚨 "We're not sure we'll add seats" (path (b) is rejected; the expansion math is at risk)
- 🚨 No saved scenario templates (G1 RED; the tool is being used for one-off reports, not institutional FP&A)

**The 5-wait-lost analysis** (per T-IR-003 Win/Loss framework): CSM tracks 5 stalled MQLs from the 5-LOI motion (T-IR-021b §4) and analyzes why each stalled. Top 3 reasons (T-INFERRED from CFO peer-review sites + G2 reviews of Anaplan/Adaptive):

1. **Lost to Adaptive Insights** (incumbent; 35-40% of stalls)
2. **Lost to Vena** (mid-market; 25-30% of stalls)
3. **"Not now"** (timing; 20-25% of stalls — these are re-targetable in Q+1)

The 5-wait-lost analysis feeds the next quarter's 5-LOI plan (re-target the "not now" cohort; deprioritize the incumbent-loss cohort unless we have a specific differentiator).

## §4. The Renewal-Conversation Script (CSM + AE, 45 min, value-summary slide)

The 45-min call structure (carry forward from T-IR-018 Value-Summary Slide template):

| Minutes | Section                       | Who leads | What                                                                                             |
| ------- | ----------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 0-15    | 3-question check-in (§1)      | CSM       | Q1 board-pack moments / Q2 dollar value / Q3 renewal path + 5-warm-intro                         |
| 15-30   | Value-summary slide co-build  | CSM       | Use T-IR-018 template; fill in Carla's 3 board-pack moments + 5 saved templates + CEO reactions  |
| 30-40   | Renewal path math walkthrough | AE        | Path (a) current seats / Path (b) +Controller seats / Path (c) multi-year — show 3-year TCO math |
| 40-45   | 5-warm-intro commitment       | CSM       | Capture the 5 names; offer the CFO reference program as incentive; confirm 30-day follow-up      |

**The 5-wins conversation is implicit in the value-summary slide** — the 3 board-pack moments, the 5 saved templates, the CEO reactions, the 3 "I taught someone" events, and the multi-year commitment together comprise the 5 wins. **The 5-warm-intro is the explicit ask in the last 5 minutes.**

**The 5-wait-lost is NOT in the renewal call** — it's a separate post-call analysis that the CSM runs with the 5-LOI motion data (T-IR-021b §4 funnel events). The 5-wait-lost is for the **next quarter's 5-LOI plan**, not this quarter's renewal.

## §5. Save Motions if YELLOW (per Churn Framework T-IR-002)

If Q3 is RED (renewal path is "we're evaluating" or "we're not sure we'll renew"):

1. **30-min Carla-only call** (not joint with AE) within 7 days. CSM listens; does not pitch.
2. **60-min joint Carla + CEO call** within 14 days if the issue is CEO-engagement (G2 RED).
3. **90-min "save squad" call** (CSM lead + AE lead + Apollo engineer + Strategos observer) within 21 days if the issue is product-fit. The save squad has a 60-min budget to identify the gap and propose a fix.
4. **Multi-year discount + CFO reference program** as last-resort incentives (T-INFERRED: 15-20% multi-year discount + quarterly Founder call; needs Founder ratification).

Per T-IR-002 churn framework, the top 3 churn reasons for ICP-1 are:

1. **"Tool becomes reporting-only"** (the E4 gate from T-IR-021a §2 was missed) — fix: re-anchor on a saved scenario template + a 30-day scenario-driven check-in
2. **"Controller left the company"** (the daily user is gone) — fix: warm-handoff to a new Controller; CSM attends the handoff meeting
3. **"CEO pulled budget"** (the strategic buyer lost faith) — fix: 60-min Carla-CEO call to re-anchor on a specific decision the CEO cares about

## §6. Day-90 Channel: Zoom + Email (NOT Slack)

**Carla-preference hypothesis:** Per JOURNEY_MAP_CARLA.md Stage 7 (Evangelism), Carla's reference-customer loyalty is built in **multi-stakeholder moments, not 1:1 moments**. CSM + AE should default to:

1. **Zoom with screen-share** (45 min, calendar-invited, Carla + AE + CSM, joint call).
2. **Email follow-up** with the value-summary slide (T-IR-018) as a PDF + the renewal path math as a 1-pager.
3. **Loom video** (5-min walkthrough of the value-summary slide) sent **after** the call as a reference for the CEO.

**Anti-pattern (DO NOT):** Async-only renewal (Loom + email, no live call). Per T-IR-002 churn framework, async-only renewal at ICP-1 has a 50% lower save-rate. CFOs need the live conversation to anchor on the value-summary; async loses the CEO-reaction moment.

**Channel escalation:** If the Zoom is declined twice, fall back to phone + email follow-up. If phone goes to voicemail, send the Loom + email + offer a 60-min in-person meeting (T-INFERRED: ICP-1 renewals at the $100K+ tier often close in-person; budget for 1 in-person AE visit per quarter per ICP-1 account).
