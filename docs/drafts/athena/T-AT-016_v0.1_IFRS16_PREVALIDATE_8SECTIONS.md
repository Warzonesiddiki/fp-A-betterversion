## §2 — ASC 842 vs IFRS 16 framework primer

**ASC 842 (US GAAP, FASB, effective 2019 public / 2022 private):** Dual model. All leases > 12 months capitalized as ROU asset + lease liability, but P&L classification splits:

- **Finance lease** (formerly capital): ROU asset depreciated + interest expense on liability (front-loaded P&L)
- **Operating lease** (US GAAP only): ROU asset amortized straight-line + **single lease expense** (smooth P&L)

**IFRS 16 (IASB, effective 2019):** Single model. All leases > 12 months capitalized. **No operating-lease distinction** — ROU asset depreciated + interest expense (front-loaded P&L for ALL leases).

**Key divergence table (D-009 Triangulation: Mimo T-MIMO-003 §6 + IFRS 16 standard + LeaseEngine.ts:132-152):**

| Dimension                               | ASC 842 (US GAAP)                                   | IFRS 16                   | FinPlan Pro impact                                           |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Single vs dual model                    | Dual (finance + operating)                          | Single (all on-BS)        | Different P&L presentation only                              |
| Discount rate                           | Implicit in lease, or IBR                           | Implicit in lease, or IBR | Same convention; IBR fallback applies                        |
| Short-term election (≤ 12 mo)           | Yes (no BPO required)                               | Yes                       | Same — both exempt                                           |
| **Low-value election (asset ≤ $5,000)** | **Yes**                                             | **No**                    | **ASC 842 gives flexibility (e.g., small office equipment)** |
| ROU asset initial direct costs          | Capitalized                                         | Capitalized               | Same                                                         |
| Lease modifications                     | ASC 842-10-25-1 separate-lease test                 | Same framework            | Same                                                         |
| Income statement (finance)              | Amortization + interest                             | Depreciation + interest   | Same                                                         |
| Income statement (operating)            | Single lease expense                                | N/A                       | Different presentation                                       |
| Variable lease payments                 | ASC 842-10-25-6 (excluded from initial measurement) | IFRS 16-27(d) (similar)   | Same                                                         |
| Sublease accounting                     | ASC 842-10-25-7 (different from head lease)         | IFRS 16 sublease test     | Subtle differences                                           |

### §6.5 — ASC 740 deferred tax (US GAAP only, IFRS equivalent is IAS 12)

**Why this matters:** ROU asset and lease liability are B/S items; under ASC 740, the difference between book lease expense (straight-line operating) and tax lease expense (accelerated deduction under IRC §162) creates a **temporary difference** that gives rise to a deferred tax asset (DTA) or deferred tax liability (DTL).

**ASC 842 operating lease — ASC 740 deferred tax treatment:**

- Book expense: $63,713/yr (straight-line, §4)
- Tax deduction: $60,000/yr in Y1 (actual cash rent paid; ASC 842 operating leases remain fully deductible as rent for US tax purposes; TCJA 2017 §199A + §163(j) limitations may apply)
- Y1 DTA: $3,713 × 21% federal rate = **$781 DTA** (book expense exceeds tax deduction, so DTA = future tax savings from the future tax deduction)
- Y2 reverses (cash $61,800 vs P&L $63,713) → DTA flips
- Cumulative DTA peak at Y3-Y4: ~$1,200 DTA (small)

**ASC 842 finance + IFRS 16 — ASC 740 / IAS 12 deferred tax treatment:**

- Book expense: $59,200 in Y1 ($14,400 interest + $44,800 amortization) vs tax deduction $60,000
- Y1 DTL: $800 × 21% = **$168 DTL** (book expense < tax deduction in Y1; reverses in Y5)
- Y5 DTL: ~$12,000 × 21% = ~$2,520 DTA flip (book expense > tax deduction in Y5)

**Verdict: Deferred tax is small in absolute terms (~$1,200 DTA peak, ~$2,500 over 5 years) and roughly equivalent across frameworks. Not material to the framework pick.**

- Witness 1 (rule): ASC 740-10-30-8 (deferred tax for B/S items) + IRC §162 (rent deduction)
- Witness 2 (data): `src/engines/LeaseEngine.ts:178-192` (the tax-line integration stub) — currently no live calc; cycle 11 P3 candidate
