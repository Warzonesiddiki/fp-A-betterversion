# Quick Start Guide - FinPlan Pro

Welcome to FinPlan Pro. This 5-minute guide will get you running your first financial model.

## 1. Import Your Chart of Accounts

Before running a budget, you need accounts.

1. Navigate to **Data & GL > Chart of Accounts**.
2. Click **Import**.
3. You can use the built-in _SaaS Template_ or upload a CSV file from QuickBooks/Xero/NetSuite.
4. Verify the total Asset/Liability/Equity mappings.

## 2. Load Actuals

1. Navigate to **Data & GL > GL Explorer**.
2. Click **Import Entries**.
3. Upload your historical Trial Balance or GL Dump.
4. The system will automatically reconcile the entries to your Chart of Accounts.

## 3. Create a Baseline Budget

1. Navigate to **Budgets > Create Budget**.
2. Name it "FY26 Base Plan".
3. The grid will auto-populate with your accounts.
4. Drag the fill handle to copy costs across months, or use the **Spread** tool to distribute an annual $120k cost evenly over 12 months.

## 4. Run a Scenario

1. Go to **Scenarios > Scenario Builder**.
2. Create a "Best Case" scenario based on your Baseline Budget.
3. Use the **Global Driver Slider** to increase Revenue assumptions by 20%.
4. Hit **Save**. The engine will instantly recalculate Net Income and Cash Flow.

## 5. View the Dashboard

1. Click **Dashboard** on the sidebar.
2. The UI will instantly display your Cash Runway, Burn Rate, and Budget-vs-Actual (BvA) variance based on your newly imported data and budget projections.

**Need Help?** Press \`Ctrl + K\` anywhere in the app to open the Command Palette and type "Help".
