# FinPlan Pro: Master Vision Statement

## 1. The Mission
To build an unbreakable, institutional-grade FP&A (Financial Planning & Analysis) application that provides **Superhuman Intelligence**.

## 2. The Benchmark
This application is designed to outperform a team of **1,000+ senior FP&A analysts**, each with **50+ years of experience**, across **all global industries**.

## 3. Core Product Philosophy
- **Deterministic Perfection:** 95% of the application's intelligence must be hardcoded into the TypeScript/Rust mathematical engines. We do not "guess" or "hallucinate."
- **Zero-Configuration Simplicity:** The end-user (who may be non-technical) simply double-clicks a single `.exe` launcher. No servers, no setup, no cloud dependencies required for core math.
- **Institutional Accuracy:** Every calculation must have a 100% audit trail. The "Perfection" mandate means zero tolerance for rounding errors or logical gaps.
- **The AI Exception:** Gemini is used strictly for **narrative translation**. It explains what the deterministic math means in plain English but is forbidden from modifying numbers.

## 4. The "10 Super Brains" Intelligence Modules
The app achieves its superhuman status through these specialized logical systems:
1. **The Unifier:** Cross-domain ripple analysis (how Inventory changes affect Debt Covenants).
2. **The Benchmark Pack:** Hardcoded "Healthy vs. Warning" logic for 15+ global sectors.
3. **The Adversary:** Continuous automated Monte Carlo stress-testing to find the "Breaking Point."
4. **The Auditor:** Real-time GAAP/IFRS compliance checking.
5. **The Strategist:** Dynamic Adversarial Decision Logic that weights advice based on the user's Risk Profile.
