## Fixing Strategy
1. **Phase 1**: Suppress test-only errors (files in `*.test.ts`) with `// @ts-nocheck` or fix mocks
2. **Phase 2**: Fix all non-test errors in order of directory: `utils/` → `hooks/` → `components/ui/` → `components/domain/` → `pages/` → `engines/` → `store/`
3. **Phase 3**: Enable strictness flags incrementally, one flag at a time

## Acceptance Criteria
- `npx tsc --noEmit` produces 0 errors
- `noUnusedLocals: true` in tsconfig
- `noUnusedParameters: true` in tsconfig
- ESLint rule `@typescript-eslint/no-explicit-any: error` active
- No new `as any` casts in PRs
