## 1. The two positioning one-liners

### For ICP-1 — "The Growing Mid-market CFO"

> **FinPlan Pro is the offline-first FP&A platform that turns your 3-week Excel scenario marathons into 30-second model runs — without your data ever leaving your laptop.**

- **Buyer (a):** CFO / VP Finance at 50–500 FTE, $10M-$100M ARR SaaS / e-commerce / professional services
- **Alternative (b):** Anaplan ($100K-$500K/yr, 6-month rollout, 5 consultants) — or, more honestly, the 3-week Excel marathon the buyer is already running
- **Price/pain anchor (c):** 3-week scenarios → 30 seconds; 10–15 day close → 3 days; $200K Anaplan bill → $20K-$100K FinPlan Pro

### For ICP-2 — "The Scrappy SaaS Controller"

> **FinPlan Pro is the open-source FP&A tool built for the controller who wears 3 hats. Set up in 30 minutes. $0 to start.**

- **Buyer (a):** Controller / first-finance-hire at 10–50 FTE, $1M-$10M ARR B2B SaaS
- **Alternative (b):** QuickBooks reports + Google Sheets shared with the CEO — or, at the upgrade ceiling, Cube's free tier
- **Price/pain anchor (c):** 2-hour Monday burn rebuild → 5 minutes; 3-day quarterly board pack → 30 minutes; $0 to try → $0 forever if it stays small

## 2. The three anti-positions (what we are NOT — and why that's the feature)

### NOT Anaplan

> "We're not Anaplan — and that's the feature. Anaplan is $100K-$500K/yr, a 6-month rollout, and 5 consultants on the invoice. We ship the same scenario modeling, the same 3-statement engine, the same multi-entity consolidation — in a binary you download tonight, with no data leaving your laptop."

- **Anchor:** Anaplan's customer-bill-of-materials (license + implementation + consultants) is the **#1 stated complaint** in FPA_COMPETITIVE_MATRIX.md. Our positioning wins by **subtracting** the friction, not by competing on feature parity.
- **Three witnesses:** (a) Mid-market CFO, (b) Anaplan at $100K-$500K/yr, (c) the 6-month rollout pain.

### NOT Pigment

> "We're not Pigment — Pigment is $50K+/yr, 14-week POCs, and they cannot run offline. We run on your laptop, ship pre-tuned for 17 sectors, and the AI Copilot is at the $0 tier — not the $50K/yr tier."

- **Anchor:** Pigment's pricing is a known shock to mid-market buyers; their offline-stance is "we are a cloud platform, that's the point." For ICP-1 in regulated industries, that **is** a feature gap, not a feature.
- **Three witnesses:** (a) Mid-market CFO with data-residency concern, (b) Pigment at $50K+/yr, (c) the EU / fintech / healthtech "data must stay on our machine" pain.

### NOT Drivetrain

> "We're not Drivetrain — Drivetrain is $30K+/yr, SaaS-only, and doesn't sync to Excel or QuickBooks. We sync to both. We also run offline. And the AI Copilot can answer 'what happens to runway if we hire 3 engineers?' in 30 seconds — try that in Drivetrain."

- **Anchor:** Drivetrain is the most-recent "Anaplan-lite" entrant; their no-Excel-sync stance is a hard line in the sand. Carlos (ICP-2) and Sandra (ICP-1) **both** still live in Excel — that's the silent incumbent. We bridge.
- **Three witnesses:** (a) Controller + CFO, (b) Drivetrain at $30K+/yr + no Excel sync, (c) the "I still need to share a .xlsx with the CEO" pain.
