# FinPlan Pro Beta Program — Mutual NDA Template

> **Status:** Legal-review-ready draft. This template is provided to the founding team's outside counsel for review before being sent to any beta customer. The terms below are deliberately balanced (no extreme one-sided clauses) to (a) protect both parties' confidential information, (b) keep the customer's data ownership unambiguous, (c) cap the liability to the $0-pricing frame of the beta.

## MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

**Effective Date:** ___________________

**Between:**

**FinPlan Pro, Inc.** ("Company"), a [Delaware] corporation with principal offices at [address] ("FinPlan Pro")

**AND**

**[Customer Legal Entity Name]** ("Customer"), a [state/country] [entity type] with principal offices at [address]

(Each a "Party," together the "Parties.")

---

### 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a beta-test arrangement in which Customer will evaluate FinPlan Pro's pre-release FP&A software platform (the "Software") for a 90-day period beginning on the Effective Date (the "Beta"). The Parties anticipate disclosing confidential information to each other in connection with the Beta.

### 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party ("Disclosing Party") to the other ("Receiving Party") in connection with the Beta, whether oral, written, visual, or electronic, that is marked or identified as confidential at the time of disclosure, or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure. Confidential Information includes, without limitation:

- **FinPlan Pro's Confidential Information:** the Software (in pre-release form), source code, product roadmaps, pricing, customer lists, financial information, AI model prompts, and the results of any benchmarking or testing of the Software.
- **Customer's Confidential Information:** Customer's financial data, business plans, customer lists, employee information, and any other non-public information Customer uploads to or generates within the Software.

### 3. Obligations

The Receiving Party shall:

(a) Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose (Section 1);
(b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except to its employees and contractors who (i) have a need to know, (ii) are bound by written confidentiality obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement, and (iii) are not competitors of the Disclosing Party;
(c) Protect the Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information of similar importance, but in no event less than reasonable care;
(d) Not copy, reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble the Software, except to the extent expressly permitted by applicable law;
(e) Promptly notify the Disclosing Party of any actual or suspected unauthorized use or disclosure.

### 4. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

(a) Was rightfully in its possession without confidentiality obligation before disclosure by the Disclosing Party;
(b) Is or becomes publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party;
(c) Was rightfully received from a third party without confidentiality obligation;
(d) Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information.

### 5. Term and Survival

This Agreement is effective on the Effective Date and continues for **12 months** thereafter (the "Term"). Each Party's obligations of confidentiality survive termination of this Agreement for an additional **12 months**.

### 6. Customer Data Ownership

As between the Parties, **Customer retains all right, title, and interest in and to Customer's data**, including any financial data, business plans, or other information Customer uploads to or generates within the Software. FinPlan Pro obtains no rights in Customer's data except the limited license to use Customer's data to provide the Software and the limited right to use **anonymized, aggregated patterns** (e.g., "the median beta customer runs 12 scenarios per week") for product development, benchmarking, and marketing.

### 7. No Warranty (Beta Software)

The Software is provided "AS-IS" and "AS-AVAILABLE" for beta evaluation. FinPlan Pro disclaims, to the maximum extent permitted by law, all warranties, express or implied, including warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, non-infringement, and accuracy. FinPlan Pro does not warrant that the Software will be uninterrupted, error-free, or secure.

### 8. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party's aggregate liability arising out of or related to this Agreement shall exceed **USD $5,000**. Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages, including lost profits or lost data, even if advised of the possibility of such damages. The foregoing limitations do not apply to (a) a Party's indemnification obligations, (b) a Party's breach of confidentiality, or (c) a Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 9. Mutual Indemnification

Each Party shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other Party from and against any third-party claims arising out of (a) the indemnifying Party's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or (b) the indemnifying Party's breach of its confidentiality obligations in Section 3. The indemnified Party shall (i) promptly notify the indemnifying Party of any claim, (ii) tender defense of the claim to the indemnifying Party, and (iii) reasonably cooperate in the defense. The indemnifying Party shall not settle any claim in a manner that imposes obligations or admissions on the indemnified Party without the indemnified Party's prior written consent.

### 10. Feedback

Customer may provide FinPlan Pro with feedback, suggestions, or recommendations regarding the Software ("Feedback"). Customer grants FinPlan Pro a non-exclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free, worldwide license to use, modify, and incorporate Feedback into the Software without restriction. Feedback will not be considered Customer's Confidential Information.

### 11. Non-Redistribution of Pre-Release Software

Customer shall not redistribute the pre-release Software, in whole or in part, to any third party. Customer may allow its employees and contractors to use the Software internally for evaluation purposes, but shall not (a) provide access to any third party (other than the Customer's authorized employees/contractors), (b) reverse-engineer the Software, (c) use the Software to provide services to third parties, or (d) use the Software in any hazardous environment or for any purpose for which it was not designed.

### 12. Termination

Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time upon 30 days' written notice. Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within 10 days of written notice, or (b) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy. Upon termination, (i) the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information of the Disclosing Party, except as required by law or document retention policies, and (ii) the Beta shall end.

### 13. General

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of [Delaware / California — TBD by counsel], without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in [County, State]. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the Beta and supersedes all prior agreements. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full force. Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other Party's prior written consent, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets. Notices shall be in writing and sent to the addresses above. This Agreement does not create any agency, partnership, or joint venture.
