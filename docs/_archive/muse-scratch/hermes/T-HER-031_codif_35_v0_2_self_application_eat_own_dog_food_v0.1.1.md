## §1 — Codif 35 v0.2 Self-Application Definition

Codif 35 v0.2 "self-application" requires every Codification spec to apply Codif 35 to
itself in at least 3 of 5 trigger conditions (TF/UC/ER/HG/**). The **"eat your own dog
food"\*\* principle: a Codif spec is only valid if it can withstand self-application without
self-contradiction.

### §1.1 — 5 Trigger Conditions (TF/UC/ER/HG/\*\*)

- **TF** (Trigger Fabrication): spec fabricates a trigger condition not actually present
- **UC** (Unverified Claim): spec asserts claim without 3-witness verification
- **ER** (Error Recovery): spec fails to specify recovery routing for errors
- **HG** (Hierarchical Gap): spec creates gaps in cross-Muse handoff protocol
- \*\*\*\* (Wildcard): spec fails self-application entirely (catastrophic)

### §1.2 — 7-Step Ritual (Required for v0.2 self-application)
