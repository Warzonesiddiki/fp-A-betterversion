## §2 — §3 3-Year Cap Rationale (4 sub-sections)

### §3.1 Cap structure (the 3-year cap is the accounting-defensible default, lifetime is open Founder question)

The §3 cap structure: **partner-sourced lead → FinPlan Pro closes → partner gets rev-share % of ACV for 3 years from contract signing.** The 3-year cap is preferred over lifetime-of-customer because: (a) ASC 606 multi-year revenue recognition is well-defined for 3-year windows (less audit friction), (b) lifetime creates forward-liability of 5-7 years that complicates FinPlan Pro's own book, (c) lifetime would force FinPlan Pro to model customer churn probability on partner-sourced deals (additional actuarial work). The lifetime alternative is **open Founder ratification per §Q3** — pending 2026-08-15 Founder-ping cycle per T-ST-019. **3-witness:** PARTNERSHIP_MOTION.md §3 Pillar 1 L99-100 + T-HER-008 v0.2 §3 rewrite (Pillar 1 default rev-share ratification) + ASC 606 industry benchmark (3-year median for SaaS channel rev-share). **[TENTATIVE on Hera legal review of "3-year vs lifetime" contract language]**
