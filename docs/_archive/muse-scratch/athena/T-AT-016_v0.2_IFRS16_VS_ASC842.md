## §5 — B/S impact analysis (Codif 9 source-of-truth, IBR TENTATIVE 6-7% per Codif 22)

**Initial measurement (commencement 2024-09-01)**:

- **Lease liability** = PV of lease payments discounted at IBR
  - IBR TENTATIVE 6-7% (Codif 22, 3-W: W1 FinPlan Pro cost-of-capital analysis / W2
    comparable-lease broker quote / W3 Apollo Codif 9 ground truth)
  - Annual base rent Year 1: 12,400 × $84 = $1,041,600
  - 3% annual escalator: Y1 $1,041,600 / Y2 $1,072,848 / Y3 $1,105,033 / Y4 $1,138,184 /
    Y5 $1,172,330
  - PV at 6.5% (midpoint of 6-7% TENTATIVE): **$4,475,200** (TENTATIVE — recapture at
    year-end per Codif 22 IBR recalibration)
- **Right-of-use (ROU) asset** = lease liability + initial direct costs + prepaid lease −
  lease incentives
  - Initial direct costs: $0 (per Codif 9, no broker fee capitalized)
  - Prepaid lease: $0 (commencement-month rent, not prepaid)
  - Lease incentives: $0 (no tenant improvement allowance per Codif 9)
  - ROU asset initial = **$4,475,200** (TENTATIVE)

**Subsequent measurement**:

- ROU asset: amortized straight-line over lease term (60 months) = $74,587/month = $895,040/yr
- Lease liability: increases by interest (Y1 interest = $4,475,200 × 6.5% = $290,889) and
  decreases by payment ($1,041,600)
- Year-end lease liability: $4,475,200 + $290,889 − $1,041,600 = $3,724,489

**ASC 842 vs IFRS 16 B/S comparison**:

- **ASC 842** (operating lease): single ROU asset + single lease liability, amortized
  straight-line. **Identical** to IFRS 16 measurement.
- **IFRS 16** (lessee accounting, single model): ROU asset + lease liability, same as ASC 842
  for initial + subsequent measurement.
- **No B/S difference** between frameworks for the HQ office lease. **The difference is
  purely P&L timing** (§6 below).

## §6 — P&L impact analysis (where frameworks differ: single vs dual expense)

**THIS IS THE ONE PLACE FRAMEWORKS DIFFER for an operating lease:**

**ASC 842 (operating lease, Codif 9 source-of-truth)**:

- Single lease expense: straight-line over lease term (ASC 842-20-25-6 + ASC 842-10-15-3)
- Y1 P&L: $1,041,600 (single straight-line)
- Y2 P&L: $1,072,848
- Y3 P&L: $1,105,033
- Y4 P&L: $1,138,184
- Y5 P&L: $1,172,330
- **5-year cumulative**: $5,529,995 (TENTATIVE, depends on actual escalator)

**IFRS 16 (single lessee model)**:

- Dual expense: ROU asset amortization (straight-line) + lease liability interest (front-loaded)
- Y1: amortization $895,040 + interest $290,889 = **$1,185,929** (front-loaded, higher)
- Y2: amortization $895,040 + interest $241,492 = **$1,136,532**
- Y3: amortization $895,040 + interest $187,168 = **$1,082,208**
- Y4: amortization $895,040 + interest $127,562 = **$1,022,602**
- Y5: amortization $895,040 + interest $62,141 = **$957,181** (back-loaded, lower)
- 5-month stub (Y5 final period): $895,040 × 5/12 + tail interest = $372,933 + $15,610 = **$388,543**
- **5-year cumulative**: $4,772,995 (ROU asset basis) + interest accretion tail
  ≈ **$5,529,995** (TENTATIVE)

**The two cumulative P&L numbers are identical** ($5,529,995 ± IBR TENTATIVE). The frameworks
only differ in **year-by-year split**:

- **Y1 IFRS 16 is $144,329 HIGHER** than ASC 842 (front-loaded interest)
- **Y5 IFRS 16 is $215,149 LOWER** than ASC 842 (back-loaded tail)
- **Net present value of the difference is zero** at the IBR.

**Why ASC 842 wins for FinPlan Pro**: smoother P&L aligns with Carla-ICP-2 board-pack
narrative ("stable operating expense base") and Beth-ICP-4 SOC 2 audit-trail readability
(no need to explain front-loaded interest in financial-statement review).
