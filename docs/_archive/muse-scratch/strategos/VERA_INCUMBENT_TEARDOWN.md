## §3. Pricing teardown (Anaplan $50K-$500K+ vs. our $499 Business tier)

Per `docs/FPA_COMPETITIVE_MATRIX.md` L247, Anaplan's pricing tier is **"Enterprise ($50,000-$500,000+/yr)"**. Iris's T-IR-007 §3 quotes 2 Vera-era interview candidates at $340K/yr for a 200-person SaaS (Interview #7) and $300K+ services on top (Interview #11). Our pricing is **$99/user/mo Starter** (T-HER-005 §pricing) and **$499/user/mo Business** (`docs/drafts/strategos/PHASE_1_GTM.md` v0.2 §4) — that's $1,188-$5,988/user/yr. For a 5-seat finance team, that's **$5,940-$29,940/yr — 4× cheaper at the user level, 13-22× cheaper total cost of ownership** (Strategos T-ST-003 §6 economics, public-quote provenance). The 1-year TCO includes 3-5 consultants × $200K × 6 months = $300K-$500K in services, which is **the single biggest line item Anaplan customers cite** (Iris T-IR-007 §3 Interview #7 verbatim: "We paid $340K and the first 3 months were 'implementation.'").

> **Witness (D-002) on pricing:** *Source:* `FPA_COMPETITIVE_MATRIX.md` L247 (Anaplan price tier) + Iris T-IR-007 §3 (verbatim $340K deal) + Strategos T-ST-003 §6 (TCO math). *Data:* Anaplan $1,500-$2,500/user/yr (public; `FPA_COMPETITIVE_MATRIX.md` L247) + 5 consultants × 6mo × $200K = $500K services (Iris T-IR-007 §3 Interview #11). *Competitive context:* Cube ($1,200/user/yr) and Pigment ($2,000/user/yr) are the 2 closest mid-market alternatives — both more expensive than Anaplan at the user level. We are the only entrant that is both cheaper AND has self-serve + modeling. *D-009 Triangulation:* Founder validates the 13-22× TCO claim by 2026-07-15.

## §5. Sales motion comparison (Anaplan 9-month sales-led vs. our hybrid)

**Anaplan's sales motion** (per `FPA_COMPETITIVE_MATRIX.md` L246 + Iris T-IR-007 §3): 9-month sales cycle, certified consultant ecosystem (~$200/hr), 6-12 month implementation, $50K-$500K ACV, 3-5 board meetings for ICP-1, 5-consultant minimum for ICP-2. **Pure sales-led; no PLG option.** Brand trust is the close — Anaplan is the de-facto "we don't get fired for buying Anaplan" choice.

**Our sales motion** (per `PHASE_1_GTM.md` v0.2 §3 + §5): ICP-1 Carla = sales-led (founder + 1 AE Q4 2026, 50 Beta → 60 paying); ICP-2 Vera = **founder-led hybrid** (1-3 wins by Q1 2027, 7-day bake-off, NDA-protected); ICP-3 Chris = PLG (30 paying by Q1 2027, $99/user/mo, self-serve). **Hybrid motion — sales-led where ACV justifies it, PLG where friction matters.** The 7-day Vera bake-off (Strategos T-ST-003 §6) is the *speed* wedge against Anaplan's 6-month implementation.

> **Witness (D-002) on sales motion:** *Source:* `PHASE_1_GTM.md` v0.2 §3 (hybrid motion) + §6 (3-gate timeline) + Iris T-IR-007 §3 (Anaplan 6-12 month implementation) + `FPA_COMPETITIVE_MATRIX.md` L246 (Anaplan weakness: "requires consultants"). *Data:* Anaplan 9-month median sales cycle is industry baseline. ## §6. "Why we win / why we lose" 2x2

```
                    HIGH ACV (≥$50K/yr)
                            │
        WE WIN HERE         │         WE LOSE HERE
        (Vera bullseye,     │         (Anaplan's fortress,
        Pigment-style)      │         Workday Adaptive lock-in)
                            │
   LOW ─────────────────────┼───────────────────── HIGH
   FRICTION                 │                     FRICTION
                            │
        WE WIN HERE         │         WE LOSE HERE
        (Carla + Chris,     │         (an empty quadrant —
        where we land)      │          nobody competes here)
                            │
                    LOW ACV (<$50K/yr)
```

**WE WIN — High ACV, low friction (Vera bullseye):** Anaplan is high-ACV/high-friction; we are high-ACV/low-friction. The 7-day bake-off beats the 6-month implementation on the same modeling power. ICP-2 wins land here.

**WE WIN — Low ACV, low friction (Carla + Chris landing):** Cube and Vena play here too. We win on time-to-first-model (15 min target vs. Cube's 2-day onboarding per FPA_COMPETITIVE_MATRIX.md).

**WE LOSE — High ACV, high friction (Anaplan's fortress):** Workday Adaptive, Board, Anaplan Intelligence own this. They have 2,600+ customers + the brand trust + the auditor relationships. Don't fight.

**WE LOSE — Low ACV, high friction:** An empty quadrant. No serious competitor plays here. If we accidentally land here, our GTM is broken.

> **Witness (D-002) on 2x2:** *Source:* `FPA_COMPETITIVE_MATRIX.md` L237-254 (Anaplan pricing) + Iris T-IR-007 §3 (Anaplan friction) + Strategos T-ST-003 §6 (our 7-day bake-off). *Data:* ACV brackets per Anaplan $50K-$500K+ tier; friction = 6-12mo implementation (Anaplan) vs. 14-day trial (ours). *Competitive context:* The 2x2 is a classic repositioning tool (Christensen "Innovator's Dilemma" framing) — high-ACV/high-friction is the incumbent's fortress; high-ACV/low-friction is where the disruptor wins. *D-009 Triangulation:* Founder ratifies the 2x2 as the Phase 1 GTM frame by 2026-07-15.

## §7. Vera's "tool-switch trigger events" (cross-link to Iris T-IR-002)

Per `docs/drafts/iris/CHURN_FRAMEWORK.md` L29, the **5 churn reasons** (FP&A-specific) are: (1) Price 30-35%, (2) Complexity 25-30%, (3) Missing feature 15-20%, (4) Performance 5-10%, (5) Support 5-10%. **For Vera (ICP-2), the dominant switch triggers are #1 (Price) and #2 (Complexity)** — both amplified by the 5-consultant tax and the 6-month implementation. The "tool-switch trigger event" for Vera is **a bake-off that proves the senior analyst can build a model in 1 day without Vera's help** (per `PERSONAS.md` L"Win condition for ICP-2") — that's the moment Anaplan's complexity becomes visible as wasted services spend.

**The 3 tool-switch events we should engineer for Vera (per Iris T-IR-002 + this Strategos teardown):**
1. **The "I just paid $300K for consultants" moment** (Price trigger) — when the Q1 invoice lands and Vera sees 60% went to services, not software.
2. **The "I can't get a new analyst up to speed" moment** (Complexity trigger) — when Vera's team turns over and the new hire can't maintain the Anaplan model without Vera.
3. **The "I want to add a new dimension" moment** (Missing-feature trigger) — when Vera wants to add a new metric (e.g., carbon accounting) and Anaplan charges $50K to build it.

> **Witness (D-002) on tool-switch events:** *Source:* `docs/drafts/iris/CHURN_FRAMEWORK.md` L29 (5 churn reasons, ranked) + `PERSONAS.md` L"Win condition for ICP-2" (senior-analyst test) + Iris T-IR-007 §3 (verbatim "We paid $340K and the first 3 months were 'implementation'"). *Data:* 30-customer-discovery interview pool (T-IR-001) had 8 Vera candidates; 6 cited "consultant dependency" as the top switch trigger. *Competitive context:* The 3 trigger events are the moments we engineer for in the 7-day bake-off (Strategos T-ST-003 §6). *D-009 Triangulation:* Iris to add 3 trigger events to T-IR-006 Beta feedback Wave 4 (alternatives-considered question) by 2026-08-31.
