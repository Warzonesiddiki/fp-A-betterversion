## §9 — Risk-Hedge Economics on $120K Floor (NEW v0.2 — deepens v0.1 §4; fixes the $120K vs $0 drift)

**Why this section:**
v0.1 §4 fire-control table had a $120K vs $0 drift (Mimo P0-4 missed it because P0-4 audited the 14mo payback math, not the 3-trigger Risk 10 math).
This section re-derives the math with full D-002 Three-Witnesses + 2-scenario clarification.

**3 trigger conditions (per T-ST-015 §5 3-trigger Risk 10, Strategos v0.1, **D-009 8th codification verified 2026-06-13** at `C:\Users\Tahir\Desktop\frontend that i want\fpa\docs\drafts\strategos\Y2_CHANNEL_CONFLICT_PREFLIGHT.md:89`):**

- **(a) Channel conflict:** 2+ direct-vs-channel overlap deals in Y1 by 2027-Q1 (Beth's clients + FinPlan Pro direct AE on same prospect = $0-$30K wasted CAC).
- **(b) Channel-NPS < Direct-NPS by >20 points for 2 consecutive quarters** (Baker Tilly-referred clients churn faster than direct-acquired = -$300K-$500K Y2 base risk).
- **(c) Baker Tilly Q1 2026 LOI conversion <15%** (vs 30-60% baseline per T-ST-015 §3 = Beth's 5-LOI commitment is hollow, and the 5 LOIs don't convert to wins = -$300K Y2 base).

**$120K per-partner floor math (2 wins × $60K ACV avg = $120K):**
If only (a) + (b) hold (not c), then Beth Y2 = 2 wins = $120K.
Y2 4-ICP total = $3.9M (base) - $300K (drop Beth from $300K) + $120K (2 wins) = **$3.72M** (Scenario A: partial channel motion, 2 wins).

**$0 per-partner floor math (channel motion defers to Y3):**
If all 3 triggers (a) + (b) + (c) hold, then Beth Y2 = 0 wins = $0.
Y2 4-ICP total = $3.9M (base) - $300K (drop Beth) = **$3.6M** (Scenario B: channel motion defers to Y3 2028, direct-only Y2).

**v0.1 §4 drift fix (the v0.2 commitment):**
v0.1 §4 said "downgrade Beth Y2 base to $120K (floor) and re-cut to direct-only Y2 ... Y2 base $3.9M → $3.6M."
This is mathematically inconsistent (the Beth column says $120K but the total column says $3.6M = Beth = $0).
v0.2 §9 splits into 2 scenarios: Scenario A ($3.72M, partial channel) and Scenario B ($3.6M, defer to Y3).
