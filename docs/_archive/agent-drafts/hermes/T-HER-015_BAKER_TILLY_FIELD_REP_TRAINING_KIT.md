## §5 — Objection Handling Cheatsheet (top 5 Baker Tilly Partner objections)

**(Drawn from T-HER-013 v0.2 §8 5 anti-patterns + §7 3 loss patterns + T-IR-021 §5 Day-7 lessons.)**

**Objection 1: "Our client uses Anaplan already — switching cost is too high."**

- **Response:** "If your client is on Anaplan and the switching cost is real, recommend Anaplan. But if the client is on Anaplan and frustrated by the 6-9 month deploy, the 2-week FinPlan Pro pilot is a low-risk evaluation. Win rate 30-40% on the speed-to-value axis per T-HER-002 §3."
- **Source:** T-HER-002 Anaplan battlecard §3 counter-table (Speed-to-Value row)

**Objection 2: "Our client is too small for FinPlan Pro."**

- **Response:** "If <5K-cell model + 1-2 finance people, the right tool might be Cube ($0-15K ACV, Google Sheets integration) or Mosaic ($20-50/user/mo, pre-built templates). Use BATTLECARD_CUBE.md or BATTLECARD_MOSAIC.md."
- **Source:** T-HER-014 v0.2 polish §7.5 Mosaic (5-15 person SaaS = Mosaic-friendly)

**Objection 3: "Our client is too big for FinPlan Pro."**

- **Response:** "If 1,000+ users + multi-dimensional hierarchies, Anaplan wins. If 500-1,000 employees with HCM choice already made, Workday multi-suite is the rational pick. If 100-500 employees mid-market with FP&A budget $80K-$200K, FinPlan Pro + best-of-breed architecture (Workday HCM + FinPlan Pro FP&A) costs 50-80% less than full multi-suite."
- **Source:** T-HER-014 v0.2 polish §7.5 Workday (mid-market sweet spot)

**Objection 4: "What about Pigment?"**

- **Response:** "Pigment = modern UX like FinPlan Pro, but cloud-only. If your client is on Pigment in a $50M+ ARR SaaS shop, stickiness is real (we lose 60-70% of those per T-HER-013 v0.2 §7 Loss pattern Y). If the client is evaluating Pigment vs FinPlan Pro, the differentiator is OLAP-on-laptop (POSITIONING.md L1) — FinPlan Pro keeps data on-device, Pigment is cloud-only."
- **Source:** T-HER-012 Pigment battlecard §3 + T-HER-013 v0.2 §7 Loss Y

**Objection 5: "Why not just use Excel + Power Query?"**

- **Response:** "If your client is a 5-10 person SaaS shop with <2K-cell model and 1 finance person, Excel + Power Query is fine — recommend it. If the client is growing (3+ finance people, >5K-cell model, audit-trail-on-AI requirement), FinPlan Pro is the right tool. The wedge opens at the 5K-cell / SOC 2 Type 2 / first-enterprise-customer boundary."
- **Source:** POSITIONING.md L1 (OLAP-on-laptop) + T-HER-013 v0.2 §7 Win B (OLAP-on-laptop 40-50% on regulated-vertical axis)
