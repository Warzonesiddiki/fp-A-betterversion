## §1 — Why Channel Works for ICP-1

**The CFOs-don't-trust-cold-emails problem:**

- ICP-1 (Carla per `iris/PERSONAS.md`) are Series B-D SaaS CFOs, 50-500 FTE, $10-100M ARR, $20-100K/yr budget, 60-90 day cycle
- They are saturated with vendor cold emails. Cold-outbound response rate to a CFO is <2% (Lemlist industry benchmark cited in `COLD_OUTBOUND_SEQUENCE.md`)
- They DO trust their auditor's tool recommendation. The auditor is the "trusted advisor" already validated in `iris/PERSONAS.md` Carla journey maps

**The Baker-Tilly-has-1,000+-SaaS-clients anchor:**

- Baker Tilly's SaaS practice alone serves 1,000+ SaaS clients in the $10-100M ARR range — the exact ICP-1 sweet spot
- A single "Advisory Partner" relationship with Baker Tilly's SaaS practice lead = warm introductions to 50-100 ICP-1 prospects/yr
- Cost to acquire: 1 partnership agreement + 1 dedicated AE = $0 incremental CAC vs. $5K-$15K CAC for direct sales

**The 10× math (per `COLD_OUTBOUND_SEQUENCE.md` benchmarks):**

- Direct sales ICP-1 yield: 30 deals/yr from 1 AE at 5% close rate on 600 outbound touches
- Channel-amplified yield (10 partners): 10 partners × 5 ICP-1 intros/yr = 50 deals/yr handled by the SAME 1 AE
- 50/30 = 1.67× volume, but the 10× comes from the COST side: $0 CAC vs. $5K-$15K CAC. So 50 deals × $5K-$15K saved = **$250K-$750K/yr in sales efficiency**
- Plus warm intros convert at 25-40% (vs. 5% cold) — so 5-8× close-rate lift on the same lead count

**The Anaplan-can't-match-this wedge** (cross-link to T-HER-002 `BATTLECARD_ANAPLAN.md`):

- Anaplan's direct sales motion is high-touch: $300K+ ACV, 6-month implementation, 5-consultant team
- They cannot economically serve a 50-person SaaS company through a partner — their deal size doesn't support partner economics
- FinPlan Pro's $299K/yr average deal size (50 seats × $499 × 12) makes partner economics work for the FIRST TIME in the FP&A category

## §3 — The "Advisory Partner" Program

**Program name:** _"FinPlan Pro Advisory Partner"_ (chosen over "Channel Partner" to signal prestige, not resale)

**4 program pillars:**

### Pillar 1 — Rev-Share (tiered: 15% / 20% / 25%)

- **Mechanics:** Partner-sourced lead → FinPlan Pro closes deal → partner gets rev-share % of ACV for **3 years from contract signing** _(lifetime-of-customer alternative is an open Founder ratification question — see Open Questions §Q3; defaulting to 3-year cap pending Founder)_
- **Payout cadence:** Quarterly, on collected revenue (not booked)
- **Clawback:** 100% clawback if customer cancels in first 90 days (incentivizes partner to bring qualified leads)
- **Example (Tier 2 default, 50-user Business deal, 3-year cap):** $299K/yr ACV × 20% × 3 years = **$179,640 cumulative LTV** per partner-sourced customer

### Pillar 2 — White-Label Option (Optional, Tier 1 only by default)

- **Mechanics:** Partner can rebrand FinPlan Pro as "[Partner] FP&A Suite" for their book of business
- **Constraint:** Brand must include "Powered by FinPlan Pro" footer (no full rebrand)
- **Pricing:** Partner sets their own price; FinPlan Pro bills partner at 50% of list (partner keeps the margin)
- **Why optional:** White-label dilutes the FinPlan Pro brand. Default is co-branded, not white-label

### Pillar 3 — Co-Marketing (3 motions)

- **Joint webinars:** Quarterly webinar series: _"FP&A Best Practices for $X-Y ARR SaaS"_ (partner as co-host, FinPlan Pro as technology sponsor)
- **Co-authored content:** 2 whitepapers/yr on the partner's SaaS practice insights + FinPlan Pro data
- **Conference presence:** Joint booth at SaaStr, FinCon, and 1 regional partner event/yr

### Pillar 4 — Dedicated Partner AE

- **1 Partner AE per Tier 1 partner, 1 Partner AE per 3 Tier 2 partners, 1 Partner AE per 5 Tier 3 partners**
- **Ratio:** ~3 Partner AEs to cover all 15 firms in the initial 15-firm list
- **Comp:** Same variable structure as direct-sales AE (30% variable on partner-sourced ACV)
- **Tooling:** Partner AE gets a PartnerStack (or Crossbeam) account for partner-sourced lead tracking

## §4 — Outreach Sequence (5 touches, 30 days)

**Target persona:** _"SaaS Practice Lead"_ (or "Partner" / "Principal" / "Director of Business Development") at the named firm. Not the CMO, not the Managing Partner — those are too senior. The Practice Lead is the operational buyer.

### Touch 1 (Day 1) — LinkedIn Connection Request

> _"Hi [First Name], I lead GTM at FinPlan Pro, the offline-first FP&A platform used by [X] SaaS CFOs. We're building an Advisory Partner program with 3 pilot firms in Q3 — Baker Tilly, Plante Moran, and BDO are on the shortlist. Would you be open to a 15-min intro to see if it's a fit?"_

**Goal:** Get connected, NOT to sell. Send a 200-character personalized note referencing their SaaS practice specifically.

### Touch 2 (Day 3-5) — Cold Email (with case study attached)

> **Subject:** "FP&A tool rec for your SaaS clients — Baker Tilly pilot"
>
> _"[First Name], following up on my LinkedIn. We just published a case study on how [FICTIONAL PLACEHOLDER — see §5 case study #1] used FinPlan Pro + their Baker Tilly advisor to cut board-pack time from 2 weeks to 4 hours. We have 3 pilot slots open for Q3 for Advisory Partners. Open to a 15-min intro? — [founder name]"_

**Attach:** 1-pager case study (the §5 case study, anonymized)

### Touch 3 (Day 10-14) — Warm Intro (via mutual connection)

**Best warm-intro sources:**

- A Beta cohort customer who is also a client of the partner firm
- A VC partner whose portfolio companies use both the partner firm AND FinPlan Pro
- A board member / advisor who knows both sides

**Mechanics:** Personal email from the warm-intro source: _"Hey [First Name], you should meet [founder name] from FinPlan Pro. We're working with them and thought of you."_

### Touch 4 (Day 18-21) — Partner AE Call (30-min Zoom)

**Agenda (30 min):**

- 5 min: Walk me through your SaaS practice. How many $10-100M ARR clients?
- 10 min: Show the FinPlan Pro + Baker Tilly case study (live walkthrough, like `SALES_DECK_ONEPAGER.md` §4)
- 10 min: Walk through the Advisory Partner program (rev-share math, co-marketing, white-label option)
- 5 min: Pilot proposal — 90-day pilot, 3-5 joint client intros, no commitment

### Touch 5 (Day 28-30) — Pilot Agreement

**Pilot structure (90 days):**

- 3-5 mutual client intros (partner-sourced, FinPlan Pro closes)
- Joint webinar delivered in month 2
- Co-authored 1-pager published in month 3
- Pilot converts to full Advisory Partner agreement OR ends with no penalty

**Decision-maker for the pilot:** SaaS Practice Lead has signing authority up to $25K. Anything above needs Managing Partner sign-off (Tier 1 only).

## §7 — Risks (3 named, with mitigations)

### Risk 1 — Partner Conflict

**Scenario:** Baker Tilly already resells Anaplan / Vena / Adaptive to their SaaS clients. They won't displace their own revenue.

**Mitigation:**

1. **AVOID Anaplan-active firms in Q3 pilot (LEAD recommendation):** Run a 30-min "competitive landscape" check (Apollo / LinkedIn Sales Navigator) on the 3 pilot firms (Deloitte / Baker Tilly / Wipfli) to confirm they don't have active Anaplan partnerships. If any do, swap to the Tier 2/3 backup list (Plante Moran / BDO / Grant Thornton / Moss Adams / Eide Bailly / Crowe / Forvis). Rationale: even an "additive" pitch is hard to land when the partner is collecting Anaplan rev-share on the same SaaS client — and a partner-conflict escalation mid-pilot is a 6-12 month setback we cannot afford in the first 3 firms.
2. **Pre-outreach diligence beyond Anaplan (supporting action):** Confirm no exclusive FP&A tooling partnership with Vena / Adaptive / Drivetrain / Pigment. The diligence script is: search the firm's "Technology Alliances" page, scan their last 2 annual reports for "preferred vendor" mentions, check LinkedIn for any partner-AE whose bio mentions a specific FP&A tool. _(Diligence template: see `BETA_PROGRAM.md` §5 NDA template + add a 1-page partner-diligence form.)_
3. **Pilot-only initially + position as additive (supporting action):** Don't ask for a "full partnership" upfront. Start with a 3-firm pilot (90 days, 3-5 mutual client intros each), expand to a full partnership AFTER the pilot converts. Frame the pitch as: _"FinPlan Pro is the offline-first, open-source complement to Anaplan for the long tail of SaaS clients that can't afford Anaplan"_ — not Anaplan-killer.

### Risk 2 — White-Label Drift

**Scenario:** Baker Tilly wants to rebrand FinPlan Pro as "Baker Tilly FP&A Suite" and remove the FinPlan Pro brand. This dilutes our brand and confuses the market.

**Mitigation:**

1. **Default to co-brand:** _"Powered by FinPlan Pro"_ footer is mandatory in white-label
2. **Brand audit clause:** Annual brand audit — if the partner's branding is causing market confusion, FinPlan Pro can require rebranding
3. **Pricing floor:** White-label partners pay 50% of list (vs. 20% rev-share for co-brand). This makes co-brand economically attractive to the partner

### Risk 3 — Support Escalation

**Scenario:** Partner-sourced customer needs support. They email FinPlan Pro support, partner AE feels bypassed, partner relationship fractures.

**Mitigation:**

1. **Named CSM per partner:** Each partner gets a named CSM (the same person for the partner AND all their sourced customers) — partner AE is always CC'd
2. **Partner Slack channel:** Dedicated Slack Connect channel per partner for real-time escalation
3. **Quarterly business review (QBR):** QBR with the partner's SaaS Practice Lead + the FinPlan Pro Partner AE + the FinPlan Pro CSM Lead. Review pipeline, closed deals, support escalations, partner satisfaction
