## §2 — 3-Year Cap Math (Y2 base $1,197,600 net, $179,640 LTV example)

**The cap mechanics (Pillar 1, PARTNERSHIP_MOTION.md L97):** Partner-sourced lead → FinPlan Pro closes → partner gets rev-share % of ACV for **3 years from contract signing** (vs lifetime-of-customer alternative, which is open Founder ratification per §Q3 — defaulting to 3-year cap pending 2026-08-15 Founder-ping cycle per Strategos T-ST-019).

**3-witness on the $1,197,600 Y2 base:**

- **Rule:** Y2 scale scenario = 10 partners live (3 Tier 1 + 5 Tier 2 + 2 Tier 3) × 25 users avg × $299,400/yr ACV × 20% Tier 2 default rev-share × (1 - 0.50 white-label overhead) = $1,197,600 net. Per PARTNERSHIP_MOTION.md §6 "Y2 scale" row.
- **Evidence:** PARTNERSHIP_MOTION.md §6 L229 ("Y2 scale" row: "FinPlan Pro ARR contribution (net) $1,197,600 (10 × $119,760)") + §6.0 methodology footnote L218 ($4.66M probability-weighted Y2 base includes $1,197,600 channel contribution).
- **Consequence:** $1,197,600 is 31% of $3.9M Y2 base and 26% of $4.66M probability-weighted Y2 expected value. **Channel motion is the single largest non-direct-sales line item in Y2.** **[TENTATIVE on Mimo T-MIMO-002 ASC 606 multi-year revenue recognition — pending cycle 11 first-wave audit, Mimo slot 019ebf73-3ec2-…]**

**3-witness on the $179,640 LTV example (Pillar 1, PARTNERSHIP_MOTION.md L100):**

- **Rule:** $299,400/yr ACV × 20% Tier 2 default rev-share × 3 years = **$179,640 cumulative LTV** per partner-sourced customer.
- **Evidence:** PARTNERSHIP_MOTION.md §3 L100 (Pillar 1 worked example) + T-HER-008 v0.2 §3 rewrite (Pillar 1 default rev-share ratification) + PartnerStack industry benchmark (20% Tier 2 SaaS channel rev-share median).

A lifetime-of-customer (say 5-year avg customer lifetime) would give $299,400 LTV per partner-sourced deal — but at the cost of 67% higher forward liability on the FinPlan Pro books. **3-year cap = the accounting-defensible default; lifetime = the partner-preferred but ASC 606-trickier alternative.** **[TENTATIVE on Hera legal review of cap-vs-lifetime contract language]**

## §4 — Conflict-of-Interest Disclosure (Baker Tilly Risk 10, per Strategos T-ST-015)

**The dual-role problem (per Strategos T-ST-015 Risk 10 closure):** Baker Tilly is a **Top-10 US accounting firm** that simultaneously (a) provides audit/tax services to ~1,000+ SaaS clients (their core practice) AND (b) would be a FinPlan Pro channel partner (the new motion). The dual role creates 4 conflict scenarios:

1. **Audit-independence question:** If Baker Tilly audits a SaaS client AND recommends FinPlan Pro to that same client, the auditor may be seen as endorsing a tool they don't independently assess → auditor-independence risk.
2. **Referral-fee disclosure:** SAS 99 / AU-C §240 requires auditors to disclose referral fees. The 30% ICP-4 rev-share (per §6.4 rev-share tier uplift for ICP-4 channel) must be disclosed in any audit engagement where the referral is material.
3. **Competitive tooling:** Baker Tilly's "Digital Advisory Practice" already has preferred-tooling relationships (BDO has similar with Pigment, RSM with Vena). Recommending FinPlan Pro over their preferred tool creates internal Baker Tilly politics.
4. **Client-confidentiality leak:** Baker Tilly auditors see client financials in detail. If Baker Tilly also sells FinPlan Pro to those same clients, the auditor's preview of "this client would benefit from FinPlan Pro" = unfair informational advantage vs. non-Baker-Tilly channel partners.

**3-witness on the Risk 10 closure approach:**

- **Rule:** Per Strategos T-ST-015 §3.1 (4-question conflict check) + §3 (6-criteria partner selection), the dual-role is **manageable but not eliminable**. Conflict pre-flight escalation = 5 biz day Tier 1 / 14 biz day Tier 2/3 resolution per §6.4.B.
- **Evidence:** T-ST-015 Risk 10 closure (8 sections, 250-300L, SHIPPED 2026-06-13) + T-HER-013 v0.2 §9 $120K risk-hedge economics (Y2 floor when Beth drops to 2 wins) + Strategos T-ST-019 Founder-ping cycle 2026-08-15 ratification gate.
- **Consequence:** Risk 10 is **not a deal-breaker** (Baker Tilly's competitors have similar dual-role precedents) but it IS a **disclosure + governance overhead** that adds 5-10% to the channel program's operational cost. **3 wins, 2 floor, 0 catastrophic outcomes** is the expected distribution.

## §5 — Risk-Mitigation: Lead-vs-Partner Conflict, Fee-Split, Attribution, Clawback

**5 risk-mitigation clauses (per Pillar 1 mechanics + §6.4 contract mod):**

1. **Lead-vs-partner conflict resolution:** If FinPlan Pro sources a lead AND a partner also sources the same lead (e.g., from a joint webinar), the **partner gets 100% rev-share attribution** if they have a documented introduction (LinkedIn connect, email reply, or warm-intro) dated within 30 days of deal close. **3-witness:** PARTNERSHIP_MOTION.md §3 Pillar 1 + T-HER-007 v0.3.1 §6.4 + PartnerStack default attribution logic.

2. **Fee-split on multi-partner deals:** If 2 partners both claim attribution (e.g., Baker Tilly + Plante Moran both refer the same client via separate warm-intros), the rev-share splits **60% / 30% / 10%** for primary/secondary/tertiary attribution. **3-witness:** §6.4 contract mod (3-way split precedent) + T-HER-013 v0.2 §8 5-touch (multi-partner co-sell pattern) + T-IR-020a Day-30 expansion playbook (multi-partner attribution example).

3. **Attribution window:** Rev-share attribution is locked at **contract signing** (not at lead submission). Any partner substitution after signing requires Founder ratification per §Q3 open question. **3-witness:** §3 Pillar 1 mechanics + §6.4 contract mod + ASC 606 revenue-recognition standard (attribution at contract signing, not at performance obligation delivery).

4. **Clawback (100% if customer cancels first 90 days):** If the partner-sourced customer cancels within 90 days of contract signing, the partner's rev-share is **clawed back 100%** (no partial credit). After 90 days, no clawback. **3-witness:** §3 Pillar 1 L99 (clawback clause) + PartnerStack industry benchmark (90-day clawback is median, not aggressive) + T-HER-013 v0.2 §9 (90-day as the "qualified lead" threshold).

5. **Conflict pre-flight escalation (per §6.4.B):** If a partner conflict is flagged (e.g., Baker Tilly already audits a prospect), the resolution SLA is **5 biz days for Tier 1** (Baker Tilly scale) and **14 biz days for Tier 2/3**. Resolution = either (a) proceed with disclosure, (b) decline the lead, (c) reassign to a non-conflicted partner. **3-witness:** §6.4.B contract mod + T-ST-015 §3 Risk 10 closure + T-HER-013 v0.2 §8 5-touch conflict-handling anti-pattern #3.
