## §1. The 3-Question Day-30 Check-in (CFO-flavored)

CSM opens Calendly, dials Carla (NOT the Controller), asks verbatim:

> **Q1 — "Walk me through your last board-pack export. What was the first question the CEO asked after seeing it?"**
>
> - **PASS:** CEO has a real, specific reaction. ("'Can we run the same numbers on the Acme deal close-miss?'" — T-INFERRED from r/FPandA peer reviews)
> - **YELLOW:** CEO saw the export, "said it looked good," no follow-up question. (Reporting-only; not yet decisions-only.)
> - **RED:** Carla hasn't exported a board pack yet, or "the CEO hasn't seen it" (Stage 5 aha missed).
>
> **Q2 — "What scenario did you run this month that you would not have run in Excel? What did you learn?"**
>
> - **PASS:** Specific + learned-something-true. ("We ran a 90% revenue-concentration scenario and the Monte Carlo showed our break-even is tighter than I thought — T-INFERRED common CFO pattern.")
> - **YELLOW:** Specific but the learning is marginal. ("I ran a what-if on hiring but it's not a real scenario yet.")
> - **RED:** "I haven't run one" or "I'm still exploring" (E4 gate from T-IR-021a is still open; intervention needed).
>
> **Q3 — "If I asked you to introduce us to one CFO peer who'd get value from this, who would you call first? Can I send you a 2-sentence blurb to forward?"**
>
> - **PASS:** Names a specific peer + says "yes, send the blurb." (The 5-LOI motion starts here.)
> - **YELLOW:** Names a peer but "let me think about it" (no commitment; 30-day re-prompt needed).
> - **RED:** "I don't have CFO peers who'd be relevant" (the 5-LOI motion is blocked; CSM needs to seed via industry-event warm intros, not Carla network).

**The 3 questions take 8-10 minutes.** The remaining 20-22 minutes are board-pack co-build + scenario walkthrough + 5-LOI plan introduction. CSM T-IR-004 §5 currently allocates 30 min for enterprise — fits. **Channel = Zoom or phone, NOT Slack DM** (vs Chris T-IR-016 §5 which is Slack-first).

## §3. The "Don't pitch the enterprise-tier" rule (per T-IR-012 §6)

Carla is enterprise by definition. The expansion motion is NOT "upgrade to enterprise tier" (Carla is already at Business or Enterprise tier at Day-30 by inference). The motion is **5-LOI peer-intro + value-anchor reinforcement**. The anti-pattern to avoid:

| Anti-pattern                              | Why it fails at Day-30                                                                | What to do instead                                                         |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Pitch the multi-year contract**         | Carla has not yet decided this is her long-term tool; pitch is premature              | Defer to Day-60 expansion conversation (T-IR-021c)                         |
| **Pitch additional scenarios/seats**      | The Controller, not Carla, is the seat-count buyer; pitching Carla confuses ownership | Address Controller with the seat math; address Carla with the value-anchor |
| **Pitch the partner-program**             | Carla is a buyer, not a partner; this is Beth's ICP-4 motion                          | Defer to Day-90 reference-customer motion, not partner motion              |
| **Pitch a CFO peer-event**                | Day-30 is too early for the evangelism ask                                            | Defer to Day-60 or Day-90 "Carla tells 2-5 peer CFOs"                      |
| **Pitch the board-pack-template-library** | Carla hasn't built her first template yet; pitch is premature                         | Defer to Day-60 saved-template conversation                                |

**The CSM T-IR-004 §5 Day-30 spec is currently 3 sentences; this section is the 5 anti-patterns that close the gap.** A 30-min call that does not avoid these 5 anti-patterns will land at a 5-10% expansion rate (T-INFERRED baseline from Anaplan/Adaptive G2 reviews). Avoiding all 5 lands at 30-40% (T-INFERRED from r/FPandA best-in-class CFOs).

## §4. The 5-LOI Motion (controller-forward path)

The 5-LOI plan at Day-30 has 4 components:

1. **5 peer-CFO names** (Carla provides; CSM pings each within 7 days with a 2-sentence blurb)
2. **5 SQL targets** (those 5 names → 5 SQLs in HubSpot by Day-37)
3. **5-warm-intro** (Carla forwards the blurb; CSM tracks open/click/response within 14 days)
4. **5-LOI draft** (CSM + Hermes T-HER-004 sales team converts 1-3 of the 5 SQLs to LOI by Day-60)

**The controller-forward path is the asymmetric advantage.** Per JOURNEY_MAP_CARLA.md Stage 6 GO WRONG, most CFO peers don't respond to cold CFO-to-CFO outreach — they respond to **Controller-to-Controller** peer intros. The motion is:

- Step 1: CSM asks Carla, "**Who is your Controller's peer at [peer company]?**" (not "who is your CFO peer")
- Step 2: Carla's Controller introduces our CSM to peer Controller (Slack/LinkedIn)
- Step 3: CSM does a 15-min "controller round-table" demo (5 Controllers on a Zoom, 30 min, low-pressure)
- Step 4: Each Controller raises their hand for a 1:1 follow-up (target: 3 of 5 → 3 SQLs)
- Step 5: Each Controller forwards to their CFO (the 5-LOI motion now has 3 SQLs from controller-forward)

**Why this works:** Controllers trust Controllers (peer relationship); CFOs trust CFOs but only after a Controller pre-vets. The Anaplan/Adaptive motion skips the controller-forward step (CFO-direct outreach), which is why their win-rate from peer-warm-intro is ~5% (T-INFERRED from G2). The controller-forward motion is our asymmetric advantage.

**The math:** If 5 LOIs are attempted at Day-30, with 30% controller-forward conversion → 1.5 SQLs by Day-37 → 0.5 LOI by Day-60. **5 LOIs at Day-30 = 0.5 LOI by Day-60 = 1 LOI by Day-90 (5-LOI quarter math).** This is the same 5-LOI math as Beth (T-IR-020a §3) and Chris (T-IR-016 §4) — the 1 LOI/quarter baseline is a Strategos T-ST-003 §6 assumption.

## §5. Day-30 Channel: Phone/Video (NOT Slack)

**Carla-preference hypothesis:** Per JOURNEY_MAP_CARLA.md Stage 6 (Habit), Carla's expansion loyalty is built in **1:1 moments, not in-app features**. CSM should default to:

1. **Zoom or phone call** (30 min, calendar-invited, Carla-only first 15 min, then optional Controller join for the 5-LOI plan walkthrough).
2. **Loom video** (5-min walkthrough of Carla's first board pack export + a sample 5-LOI blurb) sent **after** the call.
3. **Email follow-up** with a 1-page "your Day-30 expansion cliff" summary + the 5-LOI blurb template.

**Anti-pattern (DO NOT):** Slack-DM-first like Chris T-IR-016 §5. CFOs do not live in Slack. **A Slack DM to Carla is a "this person still doesn't understand my workflow" signal.** The Controller can be on Slack (and probably is); the CFO cannot.

**Channel escalation:** Same as T-IR-021a §5. If Zoom is declined twice, fall back to phone. If phone goes to voicemail, send Loom + email. If all three channels go silent for 5 business days, escalate to "save-or-lose" intervention (60-min Carla-only call, calendar-held by CSM lead).
